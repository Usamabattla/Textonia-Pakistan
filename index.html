About Us

We're on a mission to simplify yarn sourcing for textile manufacturers worldwide. Empowering businesses with premium, high-grade materials.

Our Story

TEXTONIA was established in 2002 when we started our operations from a small room with two team members and provided local sourcing services to our customers. Our core values of Integrity, Honesty, Respect, Perseverance and Responsibility fueled our business and our customers started converting into our clients. At inception, our main focus was on Polyester and Viscose Yarns and we dealt with local traders with a vision to facilitate them in their day-to-day buying.

In a very short span of time we gained plenty of trust of our clients and started to offer import sourcing services for a variety of textile raw materials including various Fibers and Fabrics. Today, with a team of highly dedicated professionals, TEXTONIA is a renowned name in textile raw material sourcing. Along with commercial importers, we have a long list of end-users who are highly satisfied with our quality and service and choose TEXTONIA over other alternatives for their raw material buying needs. We represent numerous globally recognized companies in Pakistan and help in developing linkages while bridging the gaps between international suppliers and our textile market.

We aim to go beyond our limits and add new and innovative products to our portfolio while continuing to serve as R&D partner for our esteemed clients.
