Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At TEXTONIA PAKISTAN ("we," "our," or "us"), we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our clients, website visitors, and partners. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or engage with our sourcing services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways, including:

  • Personal Data: Personally identifiable information, such as your name, company name, email address, and phone number, that you voluntarily give to us when you contact us via our website or email.
  • Derivative Data: Information our servers automatically collect when you access the website, such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, access times, and the pages you have viewed directly before and after accessing the website.

2. How We Use Your Information

Having accurate information about you permits us to provide you with a smooth, efficient, and customized experience. Specifically, we may use information collected about you via the website to:

  • Respond to your inquiries, quotes, and customer service requests.
  • Fulfill and manage orders, payments, and deliveries of textile raw materials.
  • Email you regarding your account or order.
  • Improve our website and service offerings to better serve the textile industry.
  • Send you business updates or relevant industry information (if you have opted in).

3. Disclosure of Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. We may share information we have collected about you in certain situations:

  • Service Providers: We may share your information with third parties that perform services for us or on our behalf, such as shipping companies, customs brokers, and logistics partners, to ensure your orders are delivered.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court or a government agency).

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

We may use cookies, web beacons, tracking pixels, and other tracking technologies on the website to help customize the website and improve your experience. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, but this may affect the functionality of the website.

5. Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your personal information. While we have taken reasonable steps to secure the personal information you provide to us, please be aware that despite our efforts, no security measures are perfect or impenetrable, and no method of data transmission can be guaranteed against any interception or other type of misuse.
